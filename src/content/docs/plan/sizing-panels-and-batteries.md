Start from your daily load in Wh (see [Energy and load budgets](/plan/energy-and-load-budgets/)).
Panels are the "income" of the system and the battery is the "savings account".

## Panels: the income

If your income is too low, your battery is always broke.

**Commodity panels (400-500 W) are the sweet spot worldwide.** The price difference between a
100 W panel and a 400 W panel is small, and the bigger panel has far better cost per watt,
better construction, and better long-term availability.

**Sun hours**
Most places get 4-6 "sun hours" a day on average. A 450 W panel × 5 sun hours ≈ 2.2 kWh per day.

**Tilt and shading**
Panels are very sensitive to partial shade. One tree branch can drop output by 30-50%. Always
mount where the sun's path is clear.

## Battery: the savings account

You want enough reserve to get through cloudy weather, but not so much that you pay for capacity
you never use.

**Chemistry**
LiFePO₄ (lithium iron phosphate) almost always wins off-grid: safe, long cycle life, stable
performance, and widely available as commodity packs.

**Voltage**
24 V is the best baseline for home systems. See
[Choose a system voltage](/plan/choose-a-system-voltage/).

## Simple sizing rules

| Panels (400-500 W each) | Battery | System size |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | about 2.5 kWh at 24 V | [Size B](/plan/system-sizes/#size-b-home) |
| 2 or 3 | 5-7.5 kWh | [Size C](/plan/system-sizes/#size-c-home-plus) and up |

If you run a refrigerator or a desktop PC, plan on at least two panels.

For a closer estimate from your own list of devices, use the
[System estimator](/plan/system-estimator/).
